Reverse-engineering the Bell state circuit

Core result — 1024 shots

The circuit applies an H gate (superposition) then a CNOT gate (entanglement), producing the Bell state (|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2. Measurement yields only 00 or 11, each ~50%.

Results from Qiskit AerSimulator. 00: 487, 11: 537. 01 and 10 never appear.
