Reverse-engineering the Bell state circuit

Core Result — 1024 shots

The circuit applies an H gate (superposition) then a CNOT gate (entanglement), producing the Bell state (|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2. Measurement yields only 00 or 11, each ~50%.

Results from Qiskit AerSimulator. 00: 487, 11: 537. The states 01 and 10 structurally never appear.

What this proves: Removing the CNOT gate breaks the quantum correlation, allowing the 01 state to appear while 11 disappears entirely. Conversely, swapping which qubit receives the H gate yields the exact same Bell distribution, demonstrating the symmetry of the entangled state. The shot-count comparison (100 vs 10,000) perfectly illustrates the law of large numbers in quantum measurement.
